Track experiments that can produce money before increasing tool spend.

## Offer Ladder

1. Free content: helpful posts/videos.
2. Low-friction lead magnet: AI readiness checklist.
3. Paid micro-offer: 90-minute audit call.
4. Manual audit package: 500-1000 EUR.
5. Productized report: later, only after manual validation.

## Revenue Gate

Increase tool spend only after at least one of:

- 100+ EUR/month side income
- 3+ serious client conversations
- 4+ published content pieces with clear audience signal
- repeated workflow pain that costs 5+ hours/month
